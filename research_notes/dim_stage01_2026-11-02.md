# Dimension templates, stage 0 (2026-11-02)

Branch `dim-1d` from main 57201bc. Design: research_notes/dimension_design_2026-11-02.md
(section 2.1, 6.3 and the owner's decisions). By the owner's change of order,
this branch holds stage 0 only. Stage 1 (1D) is parked on `dim-1d-stage1-wip`
(see the end of this note).

## What changed

D is a template parameter where the 3D instructions stay the same:

- `exact_predicates`: `BoxN<D>` holds one side per axis with the isotropic
  step (the rounding rule is unchanged), and `Box = BoxN<3>`. Also
  `OffsetN<D>` (`Offset = OffsetN<3>`), `Points<Memory, D>` (padded to 4 ints in
  3D), and `offset()` and `snap(exec, ...)` in D.
- `neighbor_search::CellList<Exec, D>` for D = 2, 3:
  - 2D Morton keys (`spread2`) and quadtree levels (`count / 2^(D k)`);
  - key bits `tiles + D (levels + fine_levels)`;
  - in 2D, sort buffers of its own (positions are 16 B there, and the sort
    needs 24 B per particle).
- `tet_mesh::Geometry<Memory, D>`, `CellFaces<Memory, D>` and `cells()` in
  D = 2, 3:
  - the pyramid is |A.d| / (2D), with its centroid at D/(D+1) of the way to
    the face (exactly 1/6 and 0.75 in 3D);
  - `cells()` now takes owner and neighbour views instead of the tetrahedra.
- `hydro`:
  - `State<D>`, `Vec<D>` and `Gradient<D>`;
  - `primitive`, `conserved`, `half_step`, HLL and HLLC in D;
  - `FiniteVolume<D, Exec, Riemann>` for D = 2, 3, with the radius and face
    angle per D after AREPO: `voronoi.c:1061-1063`, and `voronoi_2d.c:1309`
    for the 2D angle 0.5 L / (d/2), checked in the source.
- `voronoi::Mesh<D, Exec>` is the one interface (owner's decision 1, breaking).
  - `Mesh<3, Exec>` is the former class.
  - Callers are updated: hydro, hydro_sedov, the tests, support.hpp,
    README.

The 3D engine files (`shell_voronoi*.hpp`, `tet_*.hpp` apart from the cell
moments) are not touched beyond type names.

New CTest checks (`mesh`):

- the 2D cell list equals a host sort by (tile, Morton key, id);
- 2D `cells()` on a hand-made square lattice gives 4 faces per cell,
  area h² and the centroid at the generator (1e-15).

These are the only 2D users so far.

## Bit identity: what it took

First gate (job 31797013, the stage-0 commit e0ae7d7 against main):

- The mesh was bit-identical: every `cells_checksum`, verify 18/18, x and
  volume after 200 moving hydro steps.
- The moving hydro state U was not: momentum differed by a few ulps in
  nearly every cell after 200 steps. Each build was reproducible on its own.

Cause: FMA contraction depends on the form of the code. Where main built
vectors and states with brace initializers (`Vec3{a, b, c}`,
`State{...}`), the templated code used loops. The arithmetic is the same,
but nvcc and gcc fuse `a * b + c` into different FMAs in the two forms.
`to_lab` additionally summed `dot(w, velocity(F))` instead of main's written-out
sum.

How it was found:

- On the CPU (gcc, `-ffp-contract=fast`): with `-ffp-contract=off` both
  builds give the same bits, so the cause is contraction.
- Swapping headers placed it in the hydro: the stage 0 library with main's
  hydro is bit-identical to main.
- On the A100 the guide was the double-precision FMA/MUL/ADD counts of each
  kernel in `cuobjdump -sass hydro_sedov`:
  - with the loops, ten kernels differed from main;
  - in the moving per-face flux kernel, 5 more DFMA and 5 fewer DMUL and DADD.

The fix (commit 7e6bf0a):

- `braced` / `make_vec` / `make_state` build a `Vec<D>` or `State<D>` as a
  brace initializer `{f(0), .., f(N-1)}` from an index pack. This is one
  generic form for every D, and is main's form in 3D.
- `to_lab` is written out per D.
- The gradient's regressor array and the solved gradient stay loops, which
  is the form that gives main's op counts there.

After the fix every flux, update and velocity kernel has main's op counts.
The `gradients` kernel has 3 fewer DADD, with equal DMUL and DFMA counts. The
runs below show equal bits; why the count differs is not established.

Not fixed: on the CPU (gcc 15, OpenMP, `-ffp-contract=fast`) some modes still
differ from main in the last bits (order 0 per-cell, moving order 1 in a
32³ test). Patching one form there moved the difference to another mode.
gcc's contraction follows the code's form further than nvcc's.

## Stage 1 (parked)

`dim-1d-stage1-wip` (commit 65a9219, on a929b45; it lacks the fix 7e6bf0a):

- `sorted_mesh.hpp` is `Mesh<1>`:
  - sort with ties by id, faces at the midpoints;
  - walls by mirror gaps, AREPO's ghosts (`voronoi_1d.c:130-133`);
  - the update checks the order first and sorts only when points cross.
- `finite_volume_1d.hpp` is one fused kernel per step:
  - reflective, outflow and inflow walls;
  - LUNA's or AREPO's dt rule.
- CTests `mesh1d` and `hydro1d` pass on the CPU.
- There are a `hydro_1d` driver, a LUNA driver
  (`scripts/luna_1d_run.py`) and a comparison script.

Measured on the CPU only (OpenMP, 4 threads):

- Static uniform runs of Sod, Shu-Osher and Woodward-Colella at N = 512
  equal LUNA MC (LIMITER=1, HLLC, CFL 0.8, LUNA on the CPU) to a relative
  1e-14 to 4e-14 over the whole run. Woodward-Colella's reflective walls are
  included.
- Sod is within LUNA's L1 bounds at 128 and 256 cells, static and moving.

Not done:

- the CUDA build of the stage-1 code (one nvcc error was fixed, but the build
  was not finished);
- A100 runs, throughput, plots and references (AthenaK, LUNA MC 65536);
- rebasing it onto the fix.
