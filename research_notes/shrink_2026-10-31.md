## Per change (library lines +/- from `git log --shortstat`)

### 1. One flip planner, tried until a flip closes (b902cf3, +95 -158; fcefdf0)

`tet_repair::detail::plan` is split into `flip23` and `flip32` (each fills
a `Plan` and says whether it is valid) and tries the 2->3 flip and the
three 3->2 flips in turn. Before, the device gave up on a 2->3 flip that
did not close its cavity (stuck code `rejected`) while the host fallback
went on to the 3->2 flips; now the device does what the host did, and
the host's own copy of the rules (and its `closes` wrapper) is gone. One
`detail::list_stuck` writes stuck entries (three copies before).

Effect: the wave lattice's and sc@1e-6's first steps no longer stick
(262k: 18-19k stuck entries and 2.2-2.3 s on the host before, job
31698265; now 0, job 31701506). shells@1e-9 still sticks.

Speed: checking `closes` for every candidate inside `plan` made steps
2-2.5 % slower at 0.1 and 0.3 (bisected over the commits, job 31700901:
the whole regression sits in this commit). fcefdf0 keeps the common path
as it was: the plan kernel plans the first valid flip without the check,
calls `closes` once, and only when that fails calls `plan(..., closing =
true)` for the first candidate that closes (job 31701256: steps within
-0.8 / +0.1 / -0.3 % of 77a903b).

The voronoi_mesh test took its stuck step from the wave lattice, which no
longer sticks; it now uses shells@1e-9 and checks both `Fallback::host`
and `Fallback::rebuild` there (before, no CTest reached
`tet_flips::finish` with `cheapest`).

### 2. No whole-mesh host repair (7c65629, +38 -140)

`finish_everywhere` (copy the whole mesh to the host, repair, copy back,
renumber every edge) never ran on 77a903b: instrumented (job 31698265),
ctest's repair and voronoi_mesh tests and `voronoi_bench step 262144` at
0.03 / 0.1 / 0.3 / 1.0 spacings on wave, lattice, sc@1e-6, shells@1e-9,
jittered, uniform, clustered and halo with `--fallback=host` finished
every region at the first attempt (8 hops), with no stuck entry left. Its
two triggers are gone: a region result whose open face is not one of the
region's border faces cannot happen (host flips need both tetrahedra in
the region, local rebuilds only accept results that match the old
boundary face for face) and now throws `std::logic_error`; after two
region attempts `finish` returns the entries it could not repair
(`Stats::stuck`) and `voronoi::Mesh::update` rebuilds (it rebuilt only
with `Fallback::rebuild`/`cheapest` before). `failing` and `to_host` were
oracles only the tests and the bench used: they are `support::failing`
and `support::host_tetrahedra` now. `Stats::checked` and `unrepaired`
were never read: removed.

### 3. Outer faces of a cavity found once (881275b, +44 -44)

`outer_faces` serves `closes` and the apply kernel; `tet_mesh::same_face`
and `tet_mesh::opposite` (the corner of a tetrahedron opposite a face)
replace three spelled-out key comparisons and the search for the back
index (apply, `finish`'s border kernel). Line-neutral, one copy each.

### 4. Vector and circumcentre helpers (4c8c336, +67 -66)

`tet_mesh::row(x, i)` and `wrap(Vec3, box)` replace the
coordinate-by-coordinate nearest-image differences in `circumcentres`,
`faces`, `pyramid` and the screening of `move_screened`;
`tet_mesh::detail::sliver` is the one sliver test (the screening and
`circumcentre` had their own); `exact_predicates::circumcentre` is the
exact numerator shared by the exact cell's vertices (`set_vertex`) and
the sliver centres. Same arithmetic, same bits (the `cells_checksum` of
the bench is unchanged: 13249599784310875101 on jittered 2.1M).

### 5. Stale comments, unused accessor (59ec9d2, +11 -11)

`CellList::cell_width` was unused; comments on the owner rule (by
particle id since D3), stuck entries and `touched_entries` describe the
code again.

### 6. The host only rebuilds locally (2545a02, +20 -72)

With the shared planner the device leaves only triangles that no flip
repairs: the host's flip pass found 0-4 flips per region (shells@1e-9,
32768, CPU). The host now rebuilds the surroundings of each failing
triangle and inverted tetrahedron of the region (up to eight passes,
`detail::rebuild`, Bowyer-Watson), and the device repair that `finish`
runs afterwards does the flips. Host time on shells@1e-9 at 262k: 5.4-7.6
s for ~8.2k stuck entries (77a903b: 6.8-7.9 s for 12.3-12.7k).

### 7. Phase timing helper, from_rings keys (2dbe91a, +18 -25)

`voronoi::Mesh` timed five phases with five copies of fence/add/reset
and a dummy member; one private `lap()` now. `from_rings` sorted the
triangles of its adjacency step by hand; `sorted_face` does it.

### Merge of main 9159978 (77e3441)

One conflict in `tet_mesh::faces`: shrink's `row`/`wrap` vertex lambda
with main's fan-triangle sums without square roots (plume idea); kept
both.

## Kept, and why

- **`walk_edge` clamps.** `Kokkos::min(walk_edge(...), max_ring_length +
  1)` in `region_edges` and `represent_dropped` is a no-op on paper
  (walk_edge never returns more), but removing it made the CUDA build hang
  in `tet_flips::region_edges` (reproduced: jobs 31699272, 31699549,
  31699847, 31700027; the voronoi_mesh test timed out at 600 s, job
  31698896); with the clamp restored every check passes. Not understood;
  the kernel also does 1-byte atomics (`atomic_fetch_or/and` on the edge
  bits), whose code generation is the main suspect. Left as it was.
- **The host fallback (tet_flips).** shells@1e-9 still sticks (~3 % of
  the cells at 262k); a rebuild costs 60-94 ms there and the host 5-8 s, so
  `cheapest` rebuilds; but for a handful of stuck entries the host is ms
  against ~0.2 s at 2.1M (cleanup2, D2). Local Bowyer-Watson stays.
- **The `-1` mode of `touched_entries`** (overflowing touched list): still
  reachable (the list is a quarter of the capacity); making it impossible
  needs growth inside `finish` that saves no lines.
- **The rounded and exact per-thread cells, `Pass`, the Cells face/ring
  pool modes** in shell_voronoi: each has a user (warp fallback, start
  patch, local rebuild); no further shared code found without touching the
  kernels' register budget.
- **`closure`/`work` diagnostics in `Cells`**: written inside the kernels;
  moving them to the bench would need a second kernel variant.

## Open

- Wave lattice first step at 2.1M: the device now repairs it alone (810
  ms) instead of a 172 ms rebuild after 583 ms of repair; the rebuild also
  compacted the tetrahedra (12.7M instead of 15.0M slots), so the next
  steps were faster (10.8 vs 16.4 ms at step 2). A compaction of the
  tetrahedra after a step with many flips, or rebuilding when the repair
  runs past ~100 rounds, would recover both; not done.
- The walk_edge clamp hang above.
- tet_mesh grew by 11 lines (the helpers live there); shell_voronoi and
  the warp kernel are unchanged apart from the shared exact circumcentre.
