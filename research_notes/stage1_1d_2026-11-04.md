## Against LUNA's code, against the WIP

  point difference is 4e-14 to 1e-7, the top end at strong shocks at 2^18; every L1 ratio prints 1.000 **[M]**.
- **WIP bug, zero-gradient wall**: the ghost took the cell's half-stepped face state. LUNA's wide step pads two
  equal ghosts (`halo_local.py:16-17`, mode "edge", `muscl_hancock.py:246-247`), so the ghost's slope and half
  step vanish and its face state is the cell's W. Fixed. Reflective was right: the ghost's stencil is the mirror.
- **Point crossing** (Shu-Osher N = 1024 unregularised, `hydro1d` keeps this as a regression): the end cell, grown
  by inflow, has huge inertia; a left-going acoustic start-up pulse slows cell 1, cell 0 runs into it at t = 0.79.
  The sort then puts the small mass in the big interval and the big mass in the small one: L1 rho 1.26 against
  1e-2 static **[M]**.
- **Regularisation in 1D**: AREPO-ng has no 1D mesh (`ONEDIMS` appears only in `src/data/constants.h`), and a
  1D face has no angle, so finite_volume.hpp's criterion does not exist. Used: AREPO-ng's other branch in

  the centre displaced along the density gradient and the drift at the sound speed. r = V / 2 is the half
  length of a cell: AREPO-ng's `get_cell_radius` has no 1D case, and this is the only assumption of the kind.
  It leaves Sod unchanged (L1 equal to 4 digits, N = 8192) and removes the crossing on Shu-Osher. A few remain on
  Woodward-Colella: sorts = 1 at some N, at x ~ 0.71 near the contact **[M]**.
- The WIP's citations of `src/voronoi_1d.c` were AREPO master. Removed; LUNA or AREPO-ng are cited instead.

## Grid: 2^52 steps, not 2^30

A cell's volume comes from snapped points, its mass from fluxes, so one grid step is relative density noise
step / h: 2^-14 at N = 2^16 **[D]**. Moving Sod N = 65536 at Courant 0.8 (job 31886893) **[M]**:

| grid | L1 rho | L1 v | L1 P |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2^52 | 1.70e-5 | 3.71e-5 | 1.31e-5 |
| 2^30 | 1.92e-5 | 2.35e-5 | 1.19e-5 |

rho is 12 % better, but **v and P are worse on the fine grid**: the 2^30 noise damps the post-shock ringing of
the next section (v error between contact and shock 2.5e-5 against 8e-6) **[M]**. So the case for 2^52 here is
rho and exact uniform-flow behaviour, not all three variables. Cost: 4 B/cell more in each of two key arrays.
Reported as a choice: 30-bit noise is not a remedy to rely on, since it grows as N.

## Why moving fails where it fails

**Sod at Courant 0.8.** The error above N ~ 4096 sits between contact and shock, in the compressed gas: post-shock
ringing, |dv| ~ 4.5e-4 at N = 65536 whatever the grid **[M]**. It is not the regularisation (no effect), not
the fused step. It goes with the Courant number: v and P at 0.4 match LUNA's order 1 to 2^18 **[M]**. MUSCL-Hancock
on a Lagrangian grid sees a slowly moving shock, which I take to be the cause **[I]**, not isolated. rho stalls near
1.1e-5 at both 0.8 and 0.4 but not at AREPO's rule (3.2e-6 at 2^18, order 0.65, the contact's); cause not isolated.

**Shu-Osher.** The design note says the inflow gas stays exactly the constant post-shock state, so one large cell is
exact there. It does not: left-going acoustic waves move right in the lab at u - c = 0.69 but slower than the gas, so
they cross it, and x in [-2.8, -0.3] is disturbed at t = 1.8 **[M]** (reference: rho 3.86 to 4.06 there). All
original cells have left that region, so a handful of grown cells must hold it, and the error is the same at every
N: L1 rho 0.0370, order 0.00 **[M]**. In x > 0, the gas the cells do cover, moving beats LUNA up to N = 4096 (rho
0.17-0.55) and then stalls too (18x at 2^18): the size of that interface does not change with N, and I take it to
reflect the waves **[I]**.

**Woodward-Colella.** Passes, by 4-11x in rho up to N = 2^16, but does not converge at the top: rho stalls at 3.3-6.1e-4
under all three rules, and even rises at Courant 0.8; P is at order 0.2 at the end. The reference explains at
most ~2e-4 of it: moving 2^16 and 2^17 differ from each other by 4.7e-4 **[M]**, as much as from the reference. Error
concentrated at x = 0.75-0.80 (contact) and 0.65-0.75 in P. Cause not isolated.

### The rule against AREPO-ng

Lines of origin/Arepo2-nextgeneration-SoA (the same code on Arepo2-nextgeneration). AREPO-ng:
dt_i = CourantFac * min(rad_i / c_i', CurrentMaxTiStep_i), then the global minimum.

| item | AREPO-ng | ours (`finite_volume_1d.hpp`, `time_step()`) | |
|---|---|---|---|
| signal speed, cell term | `csnd = Csnd + |Vel - VelVertex|`, `CurrentMaxTiStep = rad / csnd`, `timestep.cc:156-165` | `r / (c + |drift|)`; drift = w - v, the regularisation | same |
| static mesh | `csnd + |Vel|`, `timestep.cc:159-160`, `sph_particle_data.h:322-324` | `r / (c + |v|)` | same; unused by default, static is LUNA's rule |
| moving, second cell term | `rad / Csnd` without |v - w|, `sph_particle_data.h:326-328, 334`: never the smaller | not computed | same result |
| pair term | `vsig = Csnd_i + Csnd_j - (Vel_j - Vel_i).dxyz / dist`, if > 0: `(dist + rad_i) / vsig`, `timestep_treebased.cc:81-88`: fluid velocities, dist between generators, dxyz from i to j | the same, side = +-1, `dist` = gap between generators | same |
| Courant factor | `All.CourantFac * ... min(...)`, `sph_particle_data.h:334` | `courant * step` | same |
| global step | `FORCE_EQUAL_TIMESTEPS`, min over bins, `timestep.cc:48-68`: rounds to a power-of-two bin | the min, no bin | known difference, as 3D |
| sound speed | `sqrt(gamma (gamma - 1) u)`, `sph_particle_data.h:210-217` | `sqrt(gamma P / rho)`, P from u | same |

Where 1D has to interpret, AREPO-ng having no 1D mesh (`ONEDIMS` only in `src/data/constants.h`):

1. **Radius.** `get_cell_radius` is `sqrt(V / pi)` for `TWODIMS` and the cube root otherwise
   (`sph_particle_data.h:280-287`), so a literal `ONEDIMS` build would take a cube root of a length. Both branches are
   the radius of a ball of the cell's volume; the 1D ball is the interval of half length V / 2, so r = V / 2.
2. **Who is a pair.** AREPO walks every point a signal can reach in the step (`timestep_treebased.cc:108-140`); ours,
   as the 3D code, the two neighbours. Tested with 32 points on each side, temporary code **[M]**: the same step
   counts to the step (Sod and Shu-Osher N = 1024, Sod 4096, WC 1024). Costs nothing here.
3. **Walls.** AREPO-ng's tree holds no point behind a wall: the end cell has no pair term on that side. Ours the same.
   A wall's ghost does not enter the time step.
4. **|v - w|.** With the face-angle criterion gone in 1D, w comes from the centroid branch (see "Against LUNA's code"),
   but the term is AREPO-ng's.

The rule itself was already right; nothing in it changed.
